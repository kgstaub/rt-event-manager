For members

A member portal they'll actually use

Their whole event, on one page

The dashboard greets each member and shows their holographic ticket, status and quick links. Everything else lives one tab away.

  • Profile with read-only .WORLD data and editable local fields
  • Emergency contacts, on hand for organisers if needed on site
  • Event tickets & “travelling with me” companions
  • Pretours, day tours and a personal event calendar
  • Order history with downloadable PDF receipts

Manage every attendee

Members edit their companions' details inline — name, phone, family, association, club, function and dietary needs — all validated and saved without leaving the page.

Bring the family

Accompanying children travel as Future Tabler / Future Lady tickets — linked to a guardian, checked against the event's age range from their date of birth, and exempt from the adult phone requirement. They join the same tours as their guardian, and get their own letters of invitation for travel.

  • Guardian linking, so every child is tied to an adult attendee
  • Date-of-birth validated against the configurable min/max age
  • A child who has outgrown the range is prompted for a full ticket

Tours & a calendar that's theirs

Pretours and day tours attach to an attendee — and can only be booked by someone who holds an event ticket, so add-ons never sell to non-attendees. Everything a member books flows into a personal event calendar alongside the official agenda.

  • One pretour per person; day tours stack unless their times clash
  • Companions and Future members can only join their guardian's tour
  • Add-ons require an event ticket — in the cart or already on the account

Registration & identity

Capture the right data, once

A WooCommerce checkout built for Round Table

The standard WooCommerce checkout is extended with Round Table fields, so you collect every attendee's details as the order is placed — no chasing forms afterwards.

  • Per-holder name, phone, family, association, club and function / role
  • Dietary needs — with an allergies detail field — for each attendee
  • Date of birth and guardian for Future Tabler / Future Lady tickets
  • Guests & partners handled cleanly, without club or association

Member data, synced live from .WORLD

Sign in with .WORLD and each member's record is pulled straight from Round Table's own identity service — so names, clubs, families and associations are always accurate and never re-typed. .WORLD stays the single source of truth.

  • Name, email, club, family and club subdomain synced on every sign-in
  • Association derived automatically from the .WORLD subdomain
  • .WORLD-owned fields stay read-only in the portal — no drift
  • All five identity providers: TABLER · 41ER · CIRCLER · AGORACLUB · TANGENTCLUB

On the day

From phone wallet to the door

Passes that stay current — or a printable PDF

Members add their ticket to Apple or Google Wallet, and when status, tours or details change the pass is pushed an update automatically. Prefer paper? The same ticket downloads as a print-ready PDF with its QR code.

Fast, signed check-in

Door staff open a mobile web app, scan the ticket QR — a signed check-in token — and mark attendance for the right session. No native app to install.

Coming soon Checking an attendee in will automatically print their name badge from the configurable badge template.

And more

Support, travel and merch — covered

Get help without leaving the portal

A “Get help” tab connects to your FreeScout helpdesk (a required dependency). Members pick a topic, attach files and chat with your team; their club, orders and tickets travel with the request.

Visa letters, verifiable

Generate a signed letter of invitation as a PDF. Each carries a QR linking to a public page that confirms it was issued.

A shop for regalia & keepsakes

Sell merch from the member portal — including a limited, numbered coin where buyers pick a free number that's theirs alone.

For staff

A management app your team runs from the front end

Graded access, right in the portal

Staff get an Event Management section inside the same portal — no wp-admin needed — with what they can see and do governed by their role:

  • Manager — full access, including “log in as member”
  • Registration manager — attendee lookup and registration duties
  • Event operator — check-in and guest profiles
  • Regular staff — a styled staff ticket, no management access

Assign a pre/day tour to a staff member as a Guide or Supervisor, and they get a “My Tours” roster with a live check-in view for their group.

Need to help someone directly? Managers and admins can log in as a member from the guest profile — see exactly what they see, fix it, and switch back safely, with no passwords exchanged.

For organisers

Run it all from wp-admin

Everything in one place

Manage tickets per order, handle refunds and transfers, curate delegates, set the hosting family, and configure wallets, badges, the helpdesk and .WORLD SSO — all inside WordPress.

  • Per-order Tickets box with stacked, editable attendee cards
  • Refunds & cancellations with confirm / decline / reactivate
  • Delegates overview with a two-per-association limit
  • Attendee badges & PDF receipts for printing

Drops into any theme

Place it with a shortcode

The portal is styled with UIkit — the framework behind YOOtheme Pro — so it feels native there, and falls back gracefully on any WordPress theme. Add each piece to a page with a shortcode:

[rt_event_manager_account]The full member account portal — set it as your WooCommerce My account page. [rt_event_manager_checkin]The staff QR check-in web app. [world_sso_login]“Sign in with .WORLD” buttons. [world_sso_register].WORLD registration form. [oauth_sso_login]Generic OAuth SSO login buttons.
